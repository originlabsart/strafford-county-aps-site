Urgent

Found an animal?

The Strafford County Animal Protection Society is not an emergency service, and a letter takes days. If an animal is hurt, lost or in danger, contact the people below.

Danger right now, to a person or an animal

Call 911.

A stray, lost or injured animal in Farmington

Start with the Farmington Police Department, 160 Main Street, Farmington.
Dispatch: (603) 755-2231 · Office: (603) 755-2731

The department says that after hours you should call the dispatch line and an officer will get back to you. Farmington Police contact page (opens in a new tab)

Elsewhere in Strafford County

Call the police department or animal control officer for the town where the animal is. Your town office can tell you who to call.

Cruelty or neglect

Report it to the local police in the town where the animal is. The New Hampshire Department of Agriculture, Markets and Food explains how: Report animal cruelty (opens in a new tab).

Injured or orphaned wildlife

In New Hampshire only licensed wildlife rehabilitators may take in wild animals. New Hampshire Fish and Game keeps the list: Licensed wildlife rehabilitators (opens in a new tab).

Your own animal is sick

Call your vet straight away. If the cost is the worry, you can write to the Society as well, but please do not wait for a reply before getting care.

Chap. VQuestions

Questions, answered.

If yours is not here, put it in a letter to the Society.

How do I ask for help with a vet bill?

Write a letter to the Society at 122 Cocheco Rd, Farmington, NH 03835. Tell it about your animal, the care your vet recommends and the estimate if you have one, and why the cost is hard. How grants work has a checklist.

How much is a grant, and is there a deadline?

The Society does not publish amounts, deadlines or an application form. Its grants are small, and each request is decided by the Society. Writing does not guarantee a grant or a set amount.

Can I phone or email?

You can call the Society on (351) 207-8729. No email address is published, so for anything in writing, please send a letter. If your animal needs care urgently, call your vet first.

Can I visit?

The address is a mailing address and there are no published opening hours. Please write before you think of visiting.

Does the Society have animals for adoption?

The Society's published purposes are grants and support: vet bills, spay and neuter, medical care, food, humane education and support for local rescues. They do not include an adoption service. If you hope to adopt, a local rescue is the place to ask.

Are gifts tax-deductible?

The Society is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, and gifts may be tax-deductible to the extent the law allows. It is classified as a private foundation, so please ask the Society, and your tax adviser, before you count on a deduction. More about giving.

Can I volunteer?

The Society does not publish volunteer roles. Write and ask how you can help.

Where can I see the Society's accounts?

Its Form 990-PF returns are public. A summary is on the About page, with a link to the returns on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer.
